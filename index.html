FAQ

Common Questions

Still have a question? Just call or WhatsApp us.

Are you really open 24 hours?

Yes — Sri Vinayaka Flex is open 24 hours, all 7 days, including festivals. Call ahead for very large orders at night.

How fast can I get my flex?

Most banners are printed the same day. For urgent orders, tell us your deadline and we'll do our best to meet it.

Can you design the banner for me?

Yes. Send your photos, text and size on WhatsApp and our team will prepare a design for your approval.

How is the price calculated?

Flex is priced by size (square feet) and material. Send us your size on WhatsApp for an exact quote.
